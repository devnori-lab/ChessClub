Club rating

How ratings work

Every member has a club rating — a number that goes up when you score better than expected and down when you score worse. It's calculated automatically from the games the committee records.

1 · Your starting rating

When you join, you answer one question: how well do you play? Your answer sets the rating you start from.

Choosing Professional puts you straight in among our top players — expect to be paired against them from your very first game.

You can change your answer until your first rated game. After that, only the committee can adjust your starting level.

2 · The formula (Elo)

After each game, both players' ratings change. First we work out how many points you were expected to score against that opponent:

E = 1 ÷ ( 1 + 10(Ropp − R) / 400 )

Then your new rating is your old rating plus K times the difference between what you scored and what you were expected to score:

Rnew = R + K × ( S − E )
R
your rating before the game; Ropp is your opponent's.
E
your expected score, between 0 and 1. Equal ratings → 0.5. Every 400 points of difference makes the stronger player 10× more likely to win.
S
what you actually scored: 1 for a win, ½ for a draw, 0 for a loss.
K
how fast ratings move: 40 for your first 30 rated games (so new players find their level quickly), then 20.

The change is rounded to the nearest whole point.

4 · What counts

  • Counts: rated over-the-board (offline) games with a real result — a win, loss or draw — in rated events or rated friendlies.
  • Doesn't count: forfeits, unfinished games, unrated events, and online games.
  • Order: games are replayed in the order they were played. If a result is corrected or a game is added late, every rating is recalculated from the start — so the numbers are always consistent.

5 · Online (Lichess) rounds

Online events are played on Lichess, which already rates every game. So online games don't change your club rating. Instead, when the committee records a Lichess game, we read both players' Lichess ratings from the game itself (the WhiteElo, BlackElo and rating-change tags in its PGN) and show your latest Lichess rating alongside your club rating.

Add your Lichess username to your profile so your online games are matched to you.
